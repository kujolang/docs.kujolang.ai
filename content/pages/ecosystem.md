# Ecosystem surfaces

These projects help people understand, operate, and present the ecosystem. They are not required to write a first Kujo program.

- [Site Kit](/ecosystem/site-kit/) is the shared design and component source kit.
- [Cinch](/ecosystem/cinch/) is a human control surface for local repositories, diffs, commands, and proof.
- [Hyperframes](/ecosystem/hyperframes/) holds campaign and launch-story assets.
- [Kujo Command](/ecosystem/command/) explores a visual command-center concept and role map.
