Workflows and approvals

Turn a sequence of steps into bounded, resumable, and auditable work.

AudiencedeveloperDifficultyintermediateStatuslaunch scopeVersioncurrentUpdated2026-08-27

Define the request with Spec, focus context with Scent, prepare execution with PackWrite, then use Dispatch for resumable steps and approvals.

Leave the workflow receipt beside the run. It should be possible to see what was requested, what was allowed, what ran, and what remains.
