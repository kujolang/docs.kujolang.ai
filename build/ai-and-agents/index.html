Build with Kujo

AI and agents

Move from a normalized model call to tools, approvals, handoffs, and local state.

SectionBuild with KujoAudiencedeveloperDifficultyintermediateStatuslaunch scopeVersioncurrent

Lead with increasing responsibility:

1. AI SDK for normalized chat and embedding contracts.

2. Agents SDK for tools, approvals, handoffs, stores, and tracing.

3. Dispatch for resumable, auditable workflows.

4. Watchdog for local request, cost, latency, error, and audit visibility.

Begin with fixture mode when a provider is not part of the question you are trying to answer. Add live credentials only at the boundary where they are needed.
