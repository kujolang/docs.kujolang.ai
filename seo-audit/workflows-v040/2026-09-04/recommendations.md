# Recommendations and measurement plan

- Submit the 98-URL sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, then inspect the workflow collection and both publishing guides.
- Resolve or intentionally replace the two anonymously unavailable GitHub destinations in `unresolved.md`.

## 7-day checks

- Record discovered/indexed status, selected canonical, impressions, clicks, and referring queries for the Publishing House Operator and Editorial publishing guides.
- Compare ChatGPT referral traffic only if analytics access exists; do not infer it from crawler access.
- Review edge logs for crawler access, 404s, and 5xx responses if logs become available.

## 28-, 60-, and 90-day comparisons

- Repeat the exact query and AI-answer benchmark rows with the same country, device, and provider conditions.
- Compare impressions, clicks, CTR, canonical selection, field CWV, backlinks, referrals, and AI citations against this dated baseline without claiming causation from correlation.

## Editorial decisions

- Keep the operator guide task-oriented and the Editorial publishing page focused on tool ownership.
- Preserve exact source/release links and the distinction between production-capable control logic and credentials/configuration required for live effects.
