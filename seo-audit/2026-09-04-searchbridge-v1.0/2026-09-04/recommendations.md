# Recommendations and measurement plan

Audit date: 2026-09-04

## Immediate after deployment

- Verify the SearchBridge guide, v1.0.0 release link, canonical, robots file, sitemap entry, JSON-LD, and tested crawler responses at the public edge.
- Keep Cloudflare and IndexNow described as fixture-only until the 1.1 review produces the explicitly required evidence.

## 7-day checks

- Inspect Search Console coverage and query/page data for the updated route if the property becomes available.
- Compare analytics referrals and engagement only against a dated pre-release baseline; do not attribute changes without sufficient evidence.

## 28-, 60-, and 90-day comparisons

- Compare impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed canonical state, external referrals, and controlled AI-answer observations using the same property, query set, country, device, and method.
- Re-run the technical crawl after material template, routing, or content changes and retain the same stable fields.

## Editorial decisions

No editorial decision is blocked. Any future promotion of a provider tier must come from SearchBridge qualification evidence, not marketing preference.
